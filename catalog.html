A NOTE ON THIS DEMO

This page is a working proof-of-concept of the circulation system: checkout, queueing, and returns are fully functional and saved to this browser. In the live product, checkouts are tied to a real member account and a real due-date system, and only one reader can hold a copy at a time across all devices, not just this one.
